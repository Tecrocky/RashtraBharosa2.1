About Royal Birds Group
=======================

Royal Birds Group Limited
-------------------------

**Royal Birds Group Limited (RBG)** is a Hong Kong–registered venture capital and holding company founded in **2017**. The company was established as a multi-sector investment platform focused on venture capital, strategic investment, business development and supporting emerging businesses.

Royal Birds Group was founded by **Dr. Vedanshii Verma, Founder**, and **Mr. Subransu Mohanty, Co-Founder**.

The company's primary identity is that of an **investment and venture capital platform**. It works around the identification, evaluation and support of businesses, entrepreneurs and emerging opportunities with the potential to develop sustainable and scalable enterprises.

Why Royal Birds Group Exists
----------------------------

Royal Birds Group was established to address an important gap faced by emerging businesses: access to capital combined with strategic business support.

The company seeks to identify businesses with viable opportunities, capable entrepreneurs and emerging areas where investment and strategic participation can contribute to long-term growth.

Its broader purpose is to connect **capital, entrepreneurship, innovation and business development** in a structured investment environment.

Rather than looking only at immediate financial returns, Royal Birds Group considers the underlying potential of a business, its ability to scale and the value it can create over the long term.

What the Company Does
---------------------

Royal Birds Group's core business is **venture capital and strategic investment**.

Its activities include identifying investment opportunities, evaluating businesses, deploying capital, supporting emerging enterprises and developing strategic business relationships.

The company can participate through different forms of investment and strategic engagement depending on the nature and requirements of an opportunity.

Its business activities include:

*   Venture capital investment
    
*   Strategic investments
    
*   Business funding
    
*   Investment evaluation and analysis
    
*   Business development
    
*   Strategic capital deployment
    
*   Support for emerging businesses
    
*   Strategic partnerships
    

The company's investment-oriented model allows it to work with businesses and entrepreneurs rather than operating as a conventional product-based or service-based company.

Investment Areas
----------------

Royal Birds Group has a broad multi-sector investment orientation.

Its areas of interest include **Technology & Artificial Intelligence, Infrastructure & Construction, Real Estate, Energy & Sustainability, Healthcare & HealthTech, Agriculture & AgriTech**, and businesses focused on India's broader consumer and enterprise markets.

Within technology, the focus can include artificial intelligence, digital platforms, automation and emerging technology businesses.

Within infrastructure and construction, the focus can include construction technology, industrial technology, logistics and related opportunities.

Healthcare, agriculture and energy provide additional areas where technology, capital and business development can contribute to changing market requirements.

The company also maintains an interest in **Bharat-focused businesses**, particularly opportunities developing products and services for India's large and diverse markets.

How Royal Birds Group Creates Value
-----------------------------------

Royal Birds Group creates value by combining investment capital with strategic assessment and business understanding.

An investment opportunity can be evaluated on the basis of:

*   Market opportunity
    
*   Business fundamentals
    
*   Business model
    
*   Scalability
    
*   Technology and innovation
    
*   Management capability
    
*   Operational strength
    
*   Governance
    
*   Strategic relevance
    
*   Long-term value creation
    

The purpose of this approach is to identify businesses that can build sustainable operations rather than relying only on short-term market opportunities.

Where appropriate, strategic relationships and capabilities can complement financial investment.

Technology and Innovation
-------------------------

Technology is an important area within Royal Birds Group's investment orientation.

The company evaluates opportunities involving emerging technologies, digital businesses, artificial intelligence, automation and technology-enabled business models.

Innovation is considered not simply as a technology feature, but as a potential driver of productivity, scalability, market access and business development.

The same approach extends to technology-enabled opportunities in sectors such as agriculture, healthcare, infrastructure and industrial businesses.

Market and Geographic Orientation
---------------------------------

Royal Birds Group originated in **Hong Kong** and developed its investment activities with an international orientation while maintaining a significant focus on India and emerging markets.

Its investment interests extend across businesses and opportunities that can operate within India as well as opportunities with broader international relevance.

Following its integration into Rashtra Bharosa Group, the company's strategic relevance is increasingly connected with India's business and investment ecosystem.

What Differentiates Royal Birds Group
-------------------------------------

Royal Birds Group's distinctive role lies in combining a venture capital perspective with a broader strategic investment approach.

The company is not limited to a single operating industry. Its multi-sector orientation allows it to evaluate opportunities across different parts of the economy and identify relationships between technology, capital and established industries.

Its value proposition is therefore centred on **capital, strategic evaluation and long-term business development**.

Corporate Journey
-----------------

Royal Birds Group was founded in **2017** as an independent Hong Kong–registered venture capital and holding company.

A significant transition took place on **11 April 2026**, when **Rashtra Bharosa Group Limited** formally acquired and registered Royal Birds Group Limited.

Following the acquisition, Royal Birds Group became part of the **Rashtra Bharosa Group** ecosystem and its venture-capital platform was integrated under the **RashBhar Capital** identity.

This marked a new stage in the company's institutional journey, bringing its investment capabilities into a broader Indian multi-sector business group.

Royal Birds Group Within Rashtra Bharosa
----------------------------------------

Within Rashtra Bharosa Group, Royal Birds Group represents the **venture capital and strategic investment function**.

Rashtra Bharosa Group operates across multiple business verticals. Royal Birds Group complements those operating businesses by providing a platform for identifying, evaluating and participating in new businesses, entrepreneurs, technologies and emerging opportunities.

The relationship creates potential connections between the Group's existing operating capabilities and external investment opportunities.

In practical terms, Royal Birds Group looks outward toward new opportunities while the Group's operating companies continue to focus on their respective businesses.

This gives Royal Birds Group a distinct strategic position within the wider Rashtra Bharosa ecosystem.

Growth and Expansion
--------------------

Royal Birds Group's growth direction is centred on expanding its investment and strategic participation across promising businesses and emerging areas.

Its expansion can involve investment, strategic partnerships, business relationships and participation in new markets where there is a clear long-term opportunity.

The emphasis remains on disciplined evaluation, sustainable business development and the ability to create lasting economic value.

Responsibility and Value Creation
---------------------------------

Royal Birds Group views responsible investment as an important part of long-term value creation.

The assessment of an opportunity therefore extends beyond financial potential and can include governance, operational capability, sustainability, responsible business practices and the broader economic contribution of a business.

The objective is to support businesses capable of creating value for investors, entrepreneurs, employees, customers and the wider economy.

Future Direction
----------------

Royal Birds Group's future direction is to develop as a long-term venture capital and strategic investment platform within the Rashtra Bharosa ecosystem.

The company seeks to build a structured investment function capable of identifying promising businesses, evaluating emerging technologies and participating in opportunities with sustainable growth potential.

Its long-term ambition is to strengthen the connection between **capital, entrepreneurship, innovation and established businesses**, while contributing to the continued development of the wider Rashtra Bharosa Group.

Royal Birds Group in One Definition
-----------------------------------

**Royal Birds Group Limited is a Hong Kong–registered venture capital and holding company founded in 2017 by Dr. Vedanshii Verma and Mr. Subransu Mohanty, focused on venture capital, strategic investment and business development, and integrated into Rashtra Bharosa Group in 2026 as part of its venture capital and strategic investment platform.**
