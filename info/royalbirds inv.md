Investment Framework
--------------------

Royal Birds Group follows a structured, evidence-led approach to investment. The purpose of the framework is to ensure that every investment opportunity is evaluated through a defined process before capital is committed.

The Group approaches investment as a combination of **commercial assessment, financial analysis, technical understanding, risk evaluation and strategic judgment**.

An investment opportunity is not considered solely on the basis of growth expectations. The underlying business, its economics, technology, market position, management capability, legal structure, operational requirements and long-term strategic relevance are examined before an investment decision is taken.

### Investment Lifecycle

The Royal Birds Group investment process is structured around a defined lifecycle:

**Opportunity Origination → Initial Screening → Detailed Evaluation → Due Diligence → Investment Round Table → Transaction Structuring → Final Approval → Investment Execution → Post-Investment Oversight**

Each stage has a specific purpose and decision threshold.

### 1\. Opportunity Origination

Investment opportunities may originate through founders, entrepreneurs, strategic relationships, referrals, market intelligence, direct proposals, sector research or opportunities identified by the Group.

At this stage, the objective is to establish whether the opportunity is relevant to the Group's investment mandate.

The initial review considers the business category, market, stage, capital requirement, ownership structure, strategic relevance and broad investment rationale.

### 2\. Initial Screening

Opportunities that meet the basic investment criteria move into an initial screening process.

The screening examines whether the business has:

*   A clearly identifiable market
    
*   A viable business model
    
*   A credible operating proposition
    
*   A reasonable path to scale
    
*   Appropriate management capability
    
*   A defined capital requirement
    
*   Potential strategic or financial value
    

Opportunities that do not meet the required initial criteria are not progressed further.

### 3\. Detailed Evaluation

For opportunities that pass the initial screening, Royal Birds Group conducts a deeper assessment.

The analysis can cover the company's financial performance, revenue model, customer base, competitive environment, market size, growth assumptions, technology, intellectual property, operational model, capital requirements and strategic position.

The objective is to convert the initial business proposition into a measurable investment case.

### 4\. Due Diligence

Before an investment proceeds, relevant due diligence is undertaken based on the nature and size of the transaction.

This may include:

**Financial Due Diligence**Revenue quality, profitability, cash flows, liabilities, working capital, financial controls, historical performance and financial projections.

**Commercial Due Diligence**Market demand, customers, competitors, pricing, distribution, market structure and growth assumptions.

**Technical Due Diligence**Technology architecture, product capability, intellectual property, technical maturity, scalability, cybersecurity and technology dependencies where relevant.

**Operational Due Diligence**Operating processes, supply chain, infrastructure, personnel, execution capability and key operational dependencies.

**Legal and Corporate Due Diligence**Ownership, corporate structure, contracts, regulatory requirements, material liabilities, intellectual property ownership and other relevant legal matters.

**Management Assessment**Founder and management capability, governance structure, execution history, organisational depth and ability to operate the business through the next stage of growth.

The level of diligence is proportionate to the complexity and risk of the proposed investment.

Investment Round Table
----------------------

The **Investment Round Table** is the central decision-making stage of the investment process.

It is designed to bring the relevant perspectives together before a transaction is recommended for approval.

The opportunity is presented as a complete investment case rather than as a standalone business pitch.

The discussion addresses:

**Business**What the company does, how it makes money, what market it serves and what creates its competitive position.

**Market**Market size, demand, competitive intensity, growth drivers and the assumptions underlying the opportunity.

**Financials**Historical performance, projected financials, cash requirements, valuation, return expectations and downside scenarios.

**Technology**Technology maturity, defensibility, scalability, intellectual property and technical execution requirements where applicable.

**Operations**Execution model, people, infrastructure, supply chain and operational dependencies.

**Management & Governance**Promoter capability, management depth, governance, reporting and accountability.

**Risk**Financial, commercial, technological, operational, regulatory and execution risks.

**Strategic Fit**The relevance of the opportunity to Royal Birds Group and, where applicable, the wider Rashtra Bharosa ecosystem.

**Transaction**Investment size, valuation, ownership, securities, rights, conditions, capital deployment and proposed exit or liquidity considerations.

The Investment Round Table is therefore not simply a meeting to approve or reject an investment. It is a structured mechanism for challenging assumptions, testing the investment thesis and determining whether the proposed transaction is justified by the available evidence.

Investment Committee Recommendation
-----------------------------------

Following the Investment Round Table, the opportunity is converted into a formal investment recommendation.

The recommendation sets out the investment thesis, transaction structure, valuation basis, principal risks, proposed protections, capital requirement and expected strategic rationale.

A decision is taken only after the material issues identified during the evaluation and Round Table process have been addressed to the required level.

Transaction Structuring
-----------------------

Where an investment is approved in principle, Royal Birds Group determines the appropriate transaction structure.

Depending on the opportunity, this may include:

**Equity Investment**Direct ownership participation in the business.

**Strategic Investment**Capital combined with a defined strategic relationship.

**Growth Capital**Investment intended to support a clearly identified expansion or scaling requirement.

**Co-Investment**Investment undertaken alongside other investors or strategic partners.

**Acquisition**Purchase of a controlling or strategic interest where the transaction requires ownership-led participation.

The structure is determined by the commercial requirements of the business, ownership objectives, valuation, risk profile and strategic purpose of the transaction.

Final Approval
--------------

No investment moves to execution until the required approvals, documentation and transaction conditions have been completed.

The final approval process considers the complete investment memorandum, diligence findings, financial model, transaction structure, risk assessment and strategic rationale.

This establishes a clear separation between **opportunity identification, evaluation, recommendation and final investment approval**.

Investment Execution
--------------------

Once approved and all required conditions are satisfied, the transaction is documented and executed.

Capital is deployed according to the agreed investment structure and transaction documents.

The investment record establishes the commercial terms, ownership position, rights and obligations, reporting requirements and other relevant conditions governing the investment.

Post-Investment Oversight
-------------------------

Royal Birds Group's involvement can continue after capital deployment.

Post-investment oversight may include periodic financial and operational reporting, performance review, strategic discussions, capital planning, governance matters and assessment of material business developments.

The purpose is not to interfere with the day-to-day management of an investee company, but to maintain appropriate visibility over the investment and ensure that agreed governance and reporting mechanisms remain effective.

Where a strategic relationship exists, opportunities for collaboration with the wider Rashtra Bharosa ecosystem may also be evaluated.

Investment Discipline
---------------------

The underlying principle of the framework is straightforward:

**Capital is committed only after the opportunity has been understood, the material risks have been examined, the investment thesis has been tested and the transaction structure has been defined.**

Royal Birds Group therefore treats investment as a **process of evidence, analysis, challenge and controlled execution**, rather than a single decision made around a table.

### Investment Decision Architecture

**Source**↓**Screen**↓**Analyse**↓**Due Diligence**↓**Investment Round Table**↓**Structure**↓**Approve**↓**Execute**↓**Monitor**

This framework provides Royal Birds Group with a repeatable basis for evaluating investments across different sectors, business models and transaction sizes while maintaining consistency in investment discipline and decision-making.
