# Reyansh Elanza
## Architectural Department

The **Architectural Department of Reyansh Elanza** is responsible for translating the development vision into a coherent architectural, spatial and visual identity for the project. The department works across concept design, planning, façade development, interiors coordination, landscape integration, technical coordination and construction-stage design support.

The architectural framework is developed in coordination with the appointed **architectural partner / design consultancy** and the internal project-development team of RashBhar Real Estate.

## 1. Architectural Vision

The architectural objective is to create a contemporary urban residential environment that combines:

**Modern architecture**  
**Functional planning**  
**High-quality spatial design**  
**Strong visual identity**  
**Climate-responsive planning**  
**Efficient building systems**  
**Long-term usability**

The design should balance a premium residential character with practical requirements such as circulation, natural light, ventilation, privacy, safety, maintenance and efficient building operations.

## 2. Architectural Partner

The project may be supported by an appointed external **architectural design partner**, working alongside RashBhar Real Estate's project and development teams.

The architectural partner's responsibilities include design development, architectural documentation, coordination with engineering consultants and support through the subsequent stages of project execution.

The final partner name, appointment scope and contractual responsibilities should be published only after formal confirmation.

## 3. Internal Architectural Function

The architectural function operates as a coordinated design department rather than as a single design activity.

Its principal responsibilities include:

**Design Direction**  
Establishing and maintaining the overall architectural language.

**Concept Development**  
Converting project requirements into an architectural concept.

**Spatial Planning**  
Developing efficient residential layouts, circulation and common spaces.

**Design Coordination**  
Coordinating architecture with structural, MEP, landscape, interior and other specialist disciplines.

**Design Documentation**  
Preparing and maintaining drawings, specifications and design information required for development and execution.

**Construction Support**  
Providing design clarification and architectural coordination during execution.

## 4. Key Architectural Disciplines

### Master Planning

The department establishes the overall spatial organisation of the development, including:

- Building placement
- Site circulation
- Vehicular movement
- Pedestrian movement
- Entry and exit planning
- Open spaces
- Shared facilities
- Service circulation
- Landscape relationships

The intention is to create a coherent relationship between built structures and open areas.

### Building Design

The architectural team develops the building form, massing and overall spatial configuration.

Key considerations include:

- Building proportions
- Floor planning
- Vertical circulation
- Core planning
- Residential efficiency
- Common areas
- Service areas
- Accessibility
- Fire and life-safety coordination

### Façade Design

The façade establishes the project's external architectural identity.

The façade design considers:

- Proportion
- Materials
- Glazing
- Shading
- Balconies
- External finishes
- Building lighting
- Weather exposure
- Maintenance requirements

The objective is to establish a contemporary appearance without compromising practical performance.

## 5. Residential Planning

Residential planning focuses on creating efficient and comfortable living environments.

The architectural team considers:

**Privacy**  
Separation between public, semi-private and private spaces.

**Natural Light**  
Appropriate daylight access throughout residential spaces.

**Ventilation**  
Planning that supports effective natural and mechanical ventilation.

**Views**  
Orientation of residential spaces toward desirable external conditions where feasible.

**Circulation**  
Clear and efficient movement within individual residences and shared areas.

**Usability**  
Layouts designed around practical daily living rather than appearance alone.

## 6. Common & Shared Spaces

Architectural planning extends beyond individual residences.

Common areas may include:

- Entrance and arrival spaces
- Lobby areas
- Corridors
- Vertical circulation areas
- Community spaces
- Recreation areas
- Amenity spaces
- Service areas
- Outdoor gathering spaces

These spaces are designed as part of the overall architectural experience rather than as separate additions.

## 7. Landscape Coordination

Landscape architecture is coordinated with the building design to create a unified site environment.

The architectural and landscape teams coordinate:

- Entry areas
- Pedestrian routes
- Open spaces
- Planting zones
- Outdoor activity areas
- Seating areas
- Hardscape
- Lighting
- Drainage
- Service access

The objective is to create functional outdoor environments while maintaining the project's overall visual identity.

## 8. Interior Architecture Coordination

The architectural department coordinates with interior-design teams where interiors form part of the project scope.

Coordination can include:

- Lobby design
- Common-area finishes
- Material palettes
- Ceiling coordination
- Lighting coordination
- Signage
- Wayfinding
- Furniture planning
- Vertical circulation finishes

The objective is to maintain consistency between the building's external architecture and its internal spaces.

## 9. Structural Coordination

Architecture must remain closely coordinated with structural engineering.

The architectural team works with structural consultants on:

- Structural grids
- Columns and walls
- Floor systems
- Core planning
- Slab requirements
- Building geometry
- Façade support
- Service zones

This coordination helps minimise conflicts between architectural intent and structural requirements.

## 10. MEP Coordination

Architecture is coordinated with mechanical, electrical and plumbing systems.

This includes:

- HVAC requirements
- Electrical rooms
- Plumbing shafts
- Fire systems
- Equipment spaces
- Service routes
- Utility areas
- Vertical risers
- Building-management systems

The objective is to integrate building services into the architecture without unnecessarily compromising usable space or design quality.

## 11. Smart Building Integration

Where smart-building systems are incorporated, the architectural department coordinates their physical integration.

Relevant systems may include:

**IoT infrastructure**  
**Building Management Systems (BMS)**  
**Digital monitoring systems**  
**Access-control infrastructure**  
**Smart lighting systems**  
**Energy-management systems**

Architectural coordination ensures that technology is integrated into the building rather than visually or functionally interfering with the design.

## 12. Sustainability in Architecture

Sustainability is considered as part of design development.

Relevant considerations may include:

- Orientation
- Daylight
- Natural ventilation
- Solar exposure
- Shading
- Energy efficiency
- Water management
- Material selection
- Landscape planning
- Operational efficiency

The specific sustainability targets and certifications should be defined at project level after formal design and engineering decisions.

## 13. BIM & Digital Design Coordination

Digital design tools can be used to coordinate architecture with structural and MEP disciplines.

A BIM-based workflow can support:

- Three-dimensional coordination
- Clash detection
- Design review
- Quantity coordination
- Drawing consistency
- Construction planning
- As-built documentation

This provides a common digital framework for multiple project disciplines.

## 14. Design Development Stages

The architectural department generally works through a structured sequence:

**Concept Design → Schematic Design → Design Development → Detailed Design → Construction Documentation → Construction Support → As-Built Coordination**

At each stage, architectural decisions are progressively converted from high-level concepts into detailed, buildable information.

## 15. Design Quality Control

Architectural quality control covers:

- Drawing review
- Design consistency
- Material coordination
- Dimensional accuracy
- Code coordination
- Consultant coordination
- Specification review
- Site design verification

The objective is to minimise design errors and maintain consistency between approved design intent and construction.

## 16. Statutory & Regulatory Coordination

The architectural function supports compliance with applicable planning and building requirements.

Coordination may include:

- Development regulations
- Building permissions
- Fire and life-safety requirements
- Accessibility requirements
- Environmental requirements where applicable
- Local authority requirements
- Project-specific statutory approvals

Final compliance remains subject to the applicable authorities and formally approved project documentation.

## 17. Construction-Stage Architectural Support

The architectural department continues to support the project during construction.

Responsibilities may include:

**Design clarifications**  
Resolving architectural queries from the construction team.

**Drawing revisions**  
Updating documentation where approved changes occur.

**Material coordination**  
Reviewing architectural materials and finishes.

**Site coordination**  
Checking that execution remains aligned with design intent.

**Technical coordination**  
Working with engineering and construction teams to resolve design interfaces.

## 18. Documentation

The architectural department is responsible for maintaining an organised design-information system.

Typical documentation includes:

- Concept drawings
- Plans
- Elevations
- Sections
- Detailed drawings
- Material specifications
- Finishes schedules
- Façade details
- Coordination drawings
- Revision records
- As-built documentation

## 19. Architectural Governance

The architectural department works within a defined approval structure.

Major design decisions should pass through appropriate review involving:

**RashBhar Real Estate Leadership**

**Structural Consultants**  
**MEP Consultants**
