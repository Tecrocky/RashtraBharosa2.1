# RashBhar HealthCare

## Nation’s Health, Our Commitment

RashBhar HealthCare is the healthcare company of **Rashtra Bharosa Group Limited**, being built to create a modern, connected and accessible healthcare ecosystem with **cardiovascular sciences at its core**.

Our long-term focus is to strengthen access to advanced cardiovascular and multi-specialty healthcare across Eastern India, connecting urban centres, regional communities and underserved areas through an integrated model of hospitals, heart-care facilities, diagnostics, technology-enabled healthcare, emergency services, preventive care, chronic-care support and long-term patient management.

RashBhar HealthCare is currently in a **development and transformation phase**.

We are not presenting ourselves as a completed hospital network. We are building one.

---

# Our Journey

The healthcare business that is today being developed as RashBhar HealthCare has its roots in **Nigam Health Care**, an earlier healthcare operation in Odisha.

Following its acquisition by Rashtra Bharosa Group in **Q2 2025**, the organisation entered a new phase of strategic transformation.

The purpose of the transition is not simply to change the name of an existing healthcare business.

It is to build a stronger institution.

RashBhar HealthCare is therefore focused on transforming existing healthcare capabilities, strengthening foundational systems, developing clinical capabilities and creating the infrastructure required for a larger healthcare network.

The history of Nigam Health Care forms part of our journey, but RashBhar HealthCare represents a new institutional direction, operating philosophy and long-term healthcare vision.

---

# Who We Are

RashBhar HealthCare is being developed as an **integrated cardiovascular healthcare company**.

Cardiovascular sciences form the centre of our clinical identity, while appropriate multi-specialty healthcare capabilities will support the broader needs of patients.

Our model is based on a simple principle:

### Cardiovascular Care at the Core.
### Healthcare Access Without Geographic Barriers.
### Technology That Strengthens Clinical Care.

We believe that healthcare should not depend solely on where a patient lives.

A person living in a major city and a person living in a rural community should both have a meaningful pathway to timely medical advice, appropriate diagnostics, specialist care, emergency support and continued follow-up.

That belief shapes the healthcare system we are building.

# Where We Are Today

RashBhar HealthCare is currently developing its healthcare platform through a combination of **acquired healthcare assets, foundational transformation and new healthcare infrastructure development**.

The organisation has acquired **Meekam Hospital**, which forms part of the healthcare platform being developed under RashBhar HealthCare.

Following this transition, the focus has moved toward restructuring, strengthening and integrating healthcare capabilities into the RashBhar HealthCare model.

At **Rajbag Healthcare**, foundational changes are currently being undertaken as part of this transformation.

A smaller existing healthcare outlet is also available within the developing network. Rather than presenting this facility as a flagship hospital, our intention is to progressively integrate such smaller-format facilities into the future **Class-C Chronic Care** framework.

This allows smaller healthcare touchpoints to contribute to chronic-care management, follow-up, monitoring and accessible community-level healthcare while more advanced cases can be connected to larger hospitals and specialist centres.

# Bridging the Urban-Rural Healthcare Gap

One of our strongest institutional commitments is to reduce the distance between patients and advanced healthcare.

Eastern India includes major urban healthcare centres as well as communities where access to specialised medical services can be more limited.

RashBhar HealthCare intends to create stronger connections between these different levels of healthcare.

Our long-term model is:

**Community → Screening → Diagnostics → Specialist Access → Hospital Care → Recovery → Home Care → Monitoring**

Telecardiology, digital healthcare, community-level services and smaller healthcare centres can help connect patients to higher levels of care when necessary.

The objective is not to bring every advanced medical service to every location.

The objective is to ensure that patients can reach the **right care at the right level**.
