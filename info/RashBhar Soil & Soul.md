# RashBhar Soil & Soul

## Agriculture Division of Rashtra Bharosa Group

**RashBhar Soil & Soul** is the **Agriculture Division of Rashtra Bharosa Group**, established to build the Group's long-term presence in agriculture and to develop modern, sustainable, science-led and commercially viable agricultural businesses in India.

The company carries forward the agricultural foundation of **Bharat Agro**, an agricultural products business with a history dating back to **2000**. Bharat Agro developed capabilities in agricultural raw materials, natural hydrocolloids, processing, manufacturing and market supply. In **Q3 2025, Bharat Agro was acquired by Rashtra Bharosa Group**, beginning a new chapter in its evolution.

Under Rashtra Bharosa Group, the business is being developed with a broader strategic purpose. The objective is not simply to continue an agricultural-products business, but to establish a dedicated Agriculture Division capable of working across **agricultural science, soil health, sustainable farming, agricultural technology, farmer development, processing, supply chains, market access and rural economic development**.

RashBhar Soil & Soul is therefore being built as an agriculture enterprise with a wider ecosystem perspective.

## Our Story

The history of RashBhar Soil & Soul begins with the agricultural foundation created through Bharat Agro.

Bharat Agro's earlier business was built around agricultural raw materials and natural hydrocolloid products, developing capabilities in processing, manufacturing, product supply and market servicing. That operating history created a foundation of experience in converting agricultural resources into commercially useful products and connecting agricultural inputs with wider markets.

The acquisition by **Rashtra Bharosa Group in Q3 2025** created the basis for a new strategic direction.

The business is now being positioned within Rashtra Bharosa's wider multi-industry ecosystem as its dedicated **Agriculture Division**, with a stronger emphasis on Indian agriculture, soil science, sustainable production, technology, value-chain development and farmer prosperity.

This transition represents an evolution:

**From agricultural products to agricultural systems.**

**From individual transactions to long-term value chains.**

**From production alone to production, processing, markets and rural prosperity.**

## What RashBhar Soil & Soul Is

RashBhar Soil & Soul is an **agriculture and soil-focused enterprise** working across the broader agricultural ecosystem.

The company focuses on the relationship between:

**Soil → Agriculture → Technology → Farmer → Processing → Supply Chain → Market → Rural Prosperity**

It is treated as an interconnected economic system in which soil quality, water availability, farming practices, technology, agricultural infrastructure, logistics, processing, market access and farmer economics all influence one another.

RashBhar Soil & Soul's role is to build businesses and capabilities around these connections.

## Why Soil & Soul

The name reflects the two fundamental dimensions of the company's purpose.

**Soil** represents the physical and biological foundation of agriculture. Soil determines how water, nutrients, roots and crops interact and therefore plays a central role in long-term agricultural productivity.

**Soul** represents the people behind agriculture: farmers, agricultural workers, farming families and rural communities.

The philosophy is therefore straightforward:

**Agricultural progress must improve both the condition of the soil and the prosperity of the people who depend upon it.**

## Flagship Technology: AgriGel™

**AgriGel™ Soil Enhancer** is being developed under RashBhar Soil & Soul as an agricultural technology and soil-enhancement R&D programme.

The current project documentation describes it as a **gel-based soil-enhancement technology** intended to investigate the interaction between engineered material and agricultural soil, particularly around moisture behaviour and the root-zone environment.

The research relationship is:

**Material → Soil → Moisture → Root Zone → Crop**

Its development includes research into soil moisture behaviour, water availability in the root zone, soil-material interaction, physical soil conditions, application efficiency and performance consistency.

The project is currently classified as **R&D / Under Development**, and its final formulation, mechanism, crop applications and commercial specifications are still being established through research and validation.

The project follows a controlled pathway:

**Lab Formulation → Soil Testing → Controlled Field Trials → Multi-Season Validation → Bio-Safety / Degradation Assessment → Commercial Scale**.

Commercialisation is intended only after technical validation, field performance, safety and compliance assessment, manufacturing feasibility, economic evaluation and market assessment.

The project's guiding principle is:

**Do not claim first. Prove first.**
