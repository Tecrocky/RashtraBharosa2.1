# RashBhar Technology
## Projects & R&D Portfolio

RashBhar Technology develops enterprise technology, artificial intelligence, digital systems and advanced robotics for real business and industrial applications. Its current technology portfolio is organized around two principal programmes: **RashtrAI** for intelligence and software systems, and **VED Ri** for embodied intelligence and robotics.

The portfolio is designed around practical industrial requirements, with particular relevance to mining, construction, manufacturing and other operational environments.

---

# 01. RashtrAI

### Industry-Focused Artificial Intelligence & Digital Workforce Platform

**Category:** Artificial Intelligence • Enterprise AI • Digital Workflows  
**Primary Focus:** Mining, Construction & Industrial Operations  
**Status:** Active Development / Internal Ecosystem Deployment

RashtrAI is RashBhar Technology's artificial intelligence and digital workflow programme. It is being developed as an industry-focused AI platform rather than as a general-purpose consumer chatbot.

The programme focuses on applying AI to enterprise workflows, operational information, workforce support and industry-specific digital processes.

Its development is initially oriented toward the Rashtra Bharosa ecosystem, allowing the technology to be developed around real operational requirements and business workflows.

### Core Areas

- Industry-specific artificial intelligence
- AI workforce systems
- Enterprise digital workflows
- Workflow automation
- Operational intelligence
- Data-driven decision support
- AI-assisted enterprise processes
- Industry-specific software systems

The central objective is to connect artificial intelligence with actual business processes so that AI functions as part of an operating environment rather than as a standalone interface.

# 02. RashtrAI Labs

### Artificial Intelligence Research & Development

**Category:** AI Research • Applied AI • Deep Technology  
**Parent Programme:** RashtrAI  
**Status:** Internal Ecosystem Deployment — v1.4

RashtrAI Labs is the research and development layer of the RashtrAI programme.

The documented research focus includes three principal areas:

### Agentic LLM Orchestration

Research into the orchestration of large language models and AI agents for structured enterprise workflows and multi-step tasks.

### Real-Time Fleet Telemetry

Development focused on processing and utilizing real-time fleet and operational telemetry for industrial environments.

### Computer Vision for Pit Safety

Research into computer-vision applications relevant to mining pit environments and safety-related operational monitoring.

The current documented active version is **v1.4 Internal Ecosystem Deployment**.

RashtrAI Labs therefore functions as the research layer behind the broader RashtrAI technology platform.

# 04. VED Ri

## Virtual Embodied Device for Robotic Intelligence

**Category:** Robotics • Embodied Intelligence • Advanced Technology R&D  
**Status:** Under Development

VED Ri is RashBhar Technology's robotics and embodied-intelligence programme.

Where RashtrAI represents **intelligence, software and AI workforce systems**, VED Ri represents **embodied intelligence, robotics and physical machines**. The two programmes are therefore complementary but technically distinct.

VED Ri is being developed around purpose-built robotic machines for industrial applications. Its development covers different physical forms according to the environment, task and operating requirement.

The current framework contains three principal variants:

1. Human Variant
2. Machinery Variant
3. Robotic Vehicle Variant

---

# 05. VED Ri — Human Variant

### Industrial Humanoid Robotics

**Category:** Robotics • Human-Form Industrial Machine  
**Status:** Active R&D  
**Position:** Flagship VED Ri R&D Project

The Human Variant is the flagship R&D direction within VED Ri.

It is conceived as an **industrial bipedal humanoid** designed around high-dexterity manipulation and industrial applications.

The development direction combines:

- Human-form robotic architecture
- Bipedal movement
- Robotic manipulation
- Physical control systems
- Software and robotics integration
- Task-specific industrial application

The Human Variant is currently described as being under active R&D. The available source material does not establish a final commercial maturity or confirmed working-production status, so those claims should not be made on the public website until technically verified.

---

# 06. VED Ri — Machinery Variant

### Industrial & Manufacturing Robotics

**Category:** Industrial Robotics • Manufacturing Automation  
**Status:** R&D

The Machinery Variant focuses on robotic systems designed around industrial machinery and manufacturing requirements.

The documented concept is based on **multi-axis articulated robotic arms for precision fabrication**.

The system direction includes applications where repeatable movement, controlled manipulation and precision fabrication are required.

Potential development areas include:

- Robotic arm architecture
- Multi-axis movement
- Precision manipulation
- Industrial fabrication
- Software-controlled robotic operation
- Task-specific industrial automation

The Machinery Variant represents the industrial-machine dimension of the VED Ri programme.

# 07. VED Ri — Robotic Vehicle Variant

### Specialised Robotic Vehicles for Industrial Environments

**Category:** Robotic Vehicles • Industrial Robotics • Mining Technology  
**Primary Application Focus:** Deep Mining

The Robotic Vehicle Variant is focused on robotic vehicles designed for specialised industrial environments.

Its first documented application focus is **deep mining**, with the concept described as a **subterranean deep-mining pit exploratory rover**.

The programme is intended to explore robotic systems capable of operating in environments where conventional human access can be difficult, restricted or operationally challenging.

The development direction includes:

- Robotic vehicle architecture
- Remote/controlled robotic operation
- Subterranean industrial environments
- Mining-oriented applications
- Exploration and operational support
- Vehicle-specific software and control systems

The deep-mining application is the first defined application focus for this VED Ri variant.

---

# 08. VED Ri Software & Control Architecture

VED Ri is not only a physical robotics programme. Each robotic form requires a corresponding software and control architecture suited to its physical configuration and intended task.

The underlying development model therefore connects:

**Software + Control Systems + Robotics Engineering + Task-Specific Development**

with the physical machine.

The Human Variant, Machinery Variant and Robotic Vehicle Variant may have different physical architectures, but each is developed around the same principle of integrating computational control with purpose-built robotic hardware.

---

# 09. VED Ri Development Framework

The documented development framework for VED Ri follows a structured sequence:

**Requirement → Design → Software / Control → Prototype → Testing → Validation → Deployment**

This framework allows the technical development of each robotic system to proceed from a defined industrial requirement toward a validated application.

The final manufacturing and deployment model is dependent on technical feasibility and R&D outcomes.

# Technology Portfolio Position

RashBhar Technology's current R&D portfolio therefore covers two connected technology layers.

**RashtrAI** develops the intelligence and software layer: AI, enterprise workflows, telemetry, agent systems and digital operational tools.

**VED Ri** develops the physical layer: robotic machines designed around specific industrial tasks and environments.

Together, these programmes represent RashBhar Technology's current direction across **Artificial Intelligence, Enterprise Software, Digital Workflows, Industrial Intelligence, Robotics and Embodied Technology**.

The documented project portfolio does not establish commercial availability for these R&D systems. Their public positioning should therefore distinguish clearly between active development, internal deployment, R&D and future application rather than presenting development projects as commercially deployed products.
