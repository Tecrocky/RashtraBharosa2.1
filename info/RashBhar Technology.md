# About RashBhar Technology

**RashBhar Technology** is the technology and innovation venture of **Rashtra Bharosa Group**, focused on building practical, intelligent and future-ready technology solutions for businesses and industries.

The company brings together **enterprise software, digital transformation, artificial intelligence, intelligent workflows, automation and emerging technology research** under one technology platform. Its objective is not simply to develop software, but to use technology to solve real operational challenges, improve productivity, strengthen decision-making and create scalable digital capabilities for the organizations it serves.

Previously known as **IndiaNova Tech**, the company has evolved into RashBhar Technology as part of Rashtra Bharosa Group's broader technology and innovation strategy.

## What is RashBhar Technology?

RashBhar Technology is a **B2B technology and innovation company** that develops business software, enterprise solutions and technology platforms designed around real organizational requirements.

The company's technology approach is centered on understanding how a business actually operates and then developing systems that can make those operations more connected, efficient, intelligent and scalable.

Its work spans three closely connected areas:

**Enterprise Technology**  
Custom B2B software, enterprise applications, digital systems and business workflow solutions.

**Artificial Intelligence**  
AI-powered platforms and intelligent digital workflows designed to bring AI into practical business and industrial operations.

**Advanced Technology & R&D**  
Research and development in automation, robotics, embodied intelligence and other emerging technologies.

This combination gives RashBhar Technology an identity that goes beyond conventional software development. It is being developed as a technology platform capable of moving from digital systems to intelligent systems and, over time, toward intelligent physical technologies.

## Why RashBhar Technology Exists

Businesses today generate enormous amounts of operational information, yet much of that information remains fragmented across people, departments, software systems and physical operations.

At the same time, many organizations continue to depend on manual processes, disconnected workflows and repetitive administrative work.

RashBhar Technology exists to address this gap.

The company seeks to bring together **people, processes, data, software and intelligence** so that organizations can operate with greater visibility, efficiency and control.

Its philosophy is straightforward:

> **Technology should solve real problems, create measurable value and become more useful as an organization grows.**

## Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence is a major strategic technology direction for RashBhar Technology.

The company is focused on applying AI to practical business and industrial environments rather than limiting AI to conventional conversational interfaces.

Its approach is to place intelligence directly into workflows where it can help people perform work, interpret information, coordinate activities and make better decisions.

This philosophy is represented by **RashtrAI**.

## RashtrAI

**RashtrAI** is an industry-specific **AI workforce and digital workflow platform** being developed within RashBhar Technology.

RashtrAI is not intended to be a general-purpose consumer AI product. Its purpose is to bring AI into actual organizational workflows and help create an intelligent digital workforce environment.

The platform is currently being developed with an initial focus on **mining and construction**, two sectors where operations involve complex workflows, field activities, workforce coordination and large volumes of operational information.

Version 1 is being used initially within the **Rashtra Bharosa ecosystem**, allowing the platform to develop through practical, real-world use.

The development philosophy is therefore:

**Build → Deploy → Learn → Improve → Scale**

This allows technology to be shaped by actual operational requirements rather than by assumptions about how organizations work.

Over time, the underlying concept of RashtrAI can be extended to additional industrial and technology sectors.

## Mining & Construction Technology

Mining and construction represent important early application environments for RashBhar Technology's AI and digital systems.

In mining, technology can support operational workflows, workforce coordination, reporting, information management and decision-support processes.

In construction, technology can support project workflows, site operations, workforce coordination, documentation, reporting and management visibility.

The objective is not to replace the operational expertise of people. Instead, technology is intended to give people better systems, better information and better tools with which to perform their responsibilities.

## VEDRI / B3

RashBhar Technology's technology vision extends beyond software and AI platforms.

One of its established advanced technology initiatives is **VEDRI — B3, Virtual Embodied Device for Robotic Intelligence**.

VEDRI represents the company's direction toward **embodied intelligence and robotics**, exploring how intelligent technology can move from digital environments into physical and robotic systems.

The broader concept connects:

**AI + Intelligence + Physical Embodiment + Robotics**

VEDRI therefore represents a longer-term R&D direction within RashBhar Technology and complements the company's software and AI development activities.
