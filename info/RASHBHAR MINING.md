WHO WE ARE
==========

A Mining Company Built Around Operations
----------------------------------------

RashBhar Mining was shaped through the acquisition of Utkal Mining in Q3 2025 and the subsequent development of a new operating platform under the RashBhar Mining identity.

The strategy is straightforward.

We look for mineral assets with strong underlying potential. We assess how they actually perform. We identify operational constraints. We modernize where required. We expand productive capacity where justified. We introduce technology where it creates measurable value. And we build the systems required to operate safely and efficiently over the long term.

This makes RashBhar Mining more than a conventional mining company.

We operate at the intersection of **asset ownership, mine operations, asset transformation, technology, processing and mineral supply**.

Our role can begin with an acquired mine, a government opportunity or a third-party operating contract. It ultimately centers on the same question:

**How can this mineral asset perform better, more safely and more responsibly?**

COMMUNITY
=========

The Mine Must Benefit the People Around It.
-------------------------------------------

A mining project is built in a real community.

There are families, homes, livelihoods, roads, schools, businesses and local expectations around every operating site.

RashBhar Mining therefore believes community development should begin **before mining begins**.

Our approach is:

**Listen → Understand → Develop → Employ → Operate Responsibly**

We want communities to participate in the economic opportunity created by mining, not simply live alongside the operation.

Local Employment First
----------------------

Where suitable opportunities exist, local people should be the first source of the mining workforce.

We seek to create opportunities across:

**Mining Operations****Equipment Operation****Maintenance****Logistics****Site Services****Administration****Support Functions**

Skills development and training can help local residents qualify for more technical and higher-value roles.

Community Needs Before Extraction
---------------------------------

Before mineral digging or large-scale mineral storage begins, community needs should already have been discussed and understood.

The development approach may include:

**Education****Healthcare****Sanitation****Local Infrastructure****Skills Development****Livelihood Support**

A working principle within RashBhar Mining’s community model is to address approximately **10–20% of identified community needs before mining starts**, subject to the specific requirements and feasibility of each project.

The objective is to ensure that development is not treated as an afterthought.

When Mining Affects Homes
-------------------------

The first responsibility is to avoid unnecessary displacement.

Where impact cannot reasonably be avoided, the company’s principle is to provide suitable re-residence or rehousing as quickly and responsibly as possible.

The standard is not simply regulatory completion.

It is responsible transition.
