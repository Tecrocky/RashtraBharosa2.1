# About RashBhar Real Estate

**RashBhar Real Estate**, previously known as **Innovex Real-Estate**, is the real-estate development vertical of **Rashtra Bharosa Group**. The business was acquired by the Group in **Q4 2025** and is focused on developing modern, high-rise luxury residential and commercial spaces across developing markets in Eastern India.

The company was established to address the growing demand for modern, high-quality and well-planned residential and commercial infrastructure in rapidly developing markets. With the acquisition of Innovex Real-Estate, Rashtra Bharosa Group brought critical assets, an experienced team and an established real-estate platform into its broader business ecosystem.

RashBhar Real Estate combines **strategic planning, data-driven analysis, technology and disciplined development** to identify opportunities and create long-term value through real estate.

## What We Do

RashBhar Real Estate operates across the real-estate development value chain, with a focus on identifying opportunities, evaluating land, planning developments and creating modern residential and commercial assets.

Its principal business areas are **premium residential development, commercial development, strategic land acquisition and technology-enabled real estate**.

The company's approach is centred on understanding changing urban requirements and developing assets that are relevant to the markets they serve.

## Premium Residential

The company focuses on creating **modern, high-quality luxury residential spaces** for urban and semi-urban families.

Its residential development approach considers location, planning, construction quality, functionality, sustainability and modern lifestyle requirements. The objective is to provide a higher standard of residential infrastructure aligned with evolving customer expectations.

## Commercial Development

RashBhar Real Estate also develops **modern commercial spaces, office environments and integrated business centres** in high-growth markets.

The focus is on creating professionally planned commercial infrastructure that supports business activity, connectivity, operational efficiency and long-term usability.

## Strategic Land Acquisition

Land acquisition is a central component of the company's business strategy.

RashBhar Real Estate evaluates land parcels across Eastern India based on their long-term development potential, connectivity, infrastructure growth, urbanisation, market demand and surrounding economic activity.

The company applies **data-driven analysis and strategic planning** to land and development decisions, with the objective of creating a disciplined and sustainable development pipeline.

## Technology Integration

Technology is incorporated into the company's approach to modern real-estate development.

RashBhar Real Estate plans to utilise technologies such as **IoT, Building Management Systems (BMS), digital monitoring and smart-building infrastructure** where appropriate.

These technologies can support efficient building operations, resource management, monitoring, maintenance and improved experiences for residents, tenants and operators.

## Data-Driven Development

The company uses data and market analysis as important inputs in its development strategy.

Market conditions, infrastructure growth, location characteristics, demographic trends and future development potential are considered when assessing opportunities.

This approach is intended to support disciplined decision-making in land acquisition, development planning and asset strategy.
