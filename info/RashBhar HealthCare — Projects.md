# RashBhar HealthCare
## Flagship Healthcare Projects

RashBhar HealthCare is building an integrated healthcare ecosystem with **cardiovascular sciences at its core**, combining advanced physical healthcare infrastructure with digital healthcare, telemedicine, specialist access, research, technology and continuous patient care.

The healthcare strategy is anchored around **two primary standalone projects**:

1. **Bhubaneswar Flagship Medical Center**
2. **Swasthya Saathi — Digital Health & Telemedicine Platform**

These are two distinct projects with different primary functions, but they are designed to operate as parts of one connected healthcare ecosystem.

The **Bhubaneswar Flagship Medical Center** is the advanced physical healthcare institution.

**Swasthya Saathi** is the digital healthcare and telemedicine platform that extends access to that ecosystem beyond the hospital.

Together, they create a model of healthcare that connects the patient journey from community access to specialist treatment, recovery and long-term monitoring.

---

# PROJECT 01
# Bhubaneswar Flagship Medical Center

## Flagship Physical Healthcare Institution

The **Bhubaneswar Flagship Medical Center** is the principal physical healthcare project of RashBhar HealthCare.

Located in **Bhubaneswar, Odisha**, it is envisioned as a **500-bed tertiary and quaternary-care medical institution**, with **cardiovascular sciences as its central clinical focus** and a comprehensive range of multi-specialty medical and surgical services.

The institution is designed to serve not only Bhubaneswar, but patients from across Odisha and, over time, the wider Eastern India region.

Its purpose is to bring together advanced medicine, critical care, diagnostics, cardiovascular expertise, digital technology, research, rehabilitation and patient-centric care within a single integrated medical campus.

### Core Identity

**500-Bed Tertiary & Quaternary Care Medical Center**

**300 Dedicated Cardiology Beds**

**Cardiovascular Sciences at the Core**

**AI-Enabled Smart Hospital**

**Advanced Emergency & Critical Care**

**Research & Innovation**

**Regional Specialist Referral Center**

**Integrated Digital & Remote Care**

---

## Vision

To build a leading healthcare institution in Eastern India delivering advanced, accessible, technology-enabled and patient-centric medical care, with cardiovascular sciences at the core.

## Mission

The Medical Center is designed to:

- Deliver advanced tertiary and quaternary healthcare.
- Establish a major cardiovascular Centre of Excellence.
- Provide comprehensive multi-specialty treatment.
- Strengthen emergency and critical-care capabilities.
- Integrate advanced technology and digital healthcare.
- Support rural and regional access to specialist medicine.
- Promote medical research and healthcare innovation.
- Provide continuous care beyond hospitalization.

# Cardiovascular Centre of Excellence

Cardiovascular medicine is the defining clinical pillar of the Medical Center.

The cardiac program is designed to cover the full patient journey:

**Prevention → Detection → Diagnosis → Intervention → Surgery → Critical Care → Recovery → Rehabilitation → Long-Term Monitoring**

### Major Cardiac Capabilities

**Cardiology**

Comprehensive diagnosis and treatment of cardiovascular diseases.

**Interventional Cardiology**

Advanced catheter-based and minimally invasive cardiovascular procedures.

**Cardiac Surgery**

Complex open-heart and cardiovascular surgical care.

**Cardiothoracic Surgery**

Advanced cardiac and thoracic surgical services.

**Heart Failure Program**

Specialized management of patients with advanced and chronic heart failure.

**Heart Transplant Program**

A planned advanced heart-transplant capability.

**LVAD Program**

Mechanical circulatory support for selected patients with advanced heart failure.

**ECMO Program**

Advanced extracorporeal support for critically ill cardiac and respiratory patients.

**Preventive Cardiology**

Risk assessment, screening and early intervention.

**Cardiac Rehabilitation**

Structured recovery and long-term cardiovascular health management.

The project's preserved planning profile includes **4 cath labs, 5 cardiac operating theatres, 2 hybrid operating theatres, a 50-bed CICU, a 20-bed CCU and ECMO capability**.

# PROJECT 02
# Swasthya Saathi

## Digital Health & Telemedicine Platform

**Swasthya Saathi** is RashBhar HealthCare's dedicated **Digital Health and Telemedicine platform**.

While the Bhubaneswar Flagship Medical Center is a physical hospital, Swasthya Saathi is the digital platform designed to bring healthcare access closer to patients wherever they are.

Its fundamental purpose is to reduce the geographical barrier between patients and healthcare professionals.

### Core Identity

**Digital Healthcare Platform**

**Telemedicine**

**Telecardiology**

**Remote Monitoring**

**Digital Patient Access**

**Referral & Care Coordination**

**Connected Healthcare**

---

# Vision

### “Bringing Quality Healthcare Closer to Every Patient.”

Swasthya Saathi is designed around a simple principle:

**A patient's location should not determine their access to appropriate medical care.**

The platform is intended to connect patients to doctors, specialists, diagnostics, hospitals and long-term healthcare services through one connected digital ecosystem.

---

# Core Healthcare Model

Swasthya Saathi follows the broader RashBhar HealthCare care model:

**Prevent → Detect → Diagnose → Treat → Recover → Monitor**

The platform is especially important in the **detect, diagnose, recover and monitor** stages.

# Overall Strategic Vision

RashBhar HealthCare's objective is to build a healthcare model in which the hospital is not isolated from the community and the digital platform is not isolated from clinical care.

The two projects therefore form one connected architecture:

**Swasthya Saathi brings patients into the healthcare system.**

**The Bhubaneswar Flagship Medical Center provides advanced treatment when that level of care is required.**

**Swasthya Saathi then helps maintain the patient's connection with healthcare after treatment.**

This creates a continuous healthcare cycle:

### PREVENT
Preventive care and health awareness

### DETECT
Early identification of health risks

### DIAGNOSE
Digital and physical diagnostics

### TREAT
Specialist and advanced hospital care

### RECOVER
Rehabilitation and post-treatment care

### MONITOR
Remote monitoring and long-term follow-up

# Final Definition

**RashBhar HealthCare's flagship healthcare strategy is built around two primary projects: the Bhubaneswar Flagship Medical Center and Swasthya Saathi.**

The **Bhubaneswar Flagship Medical Center** is envisioned as a 500-bed advanced tertiary and quaternary-care medical institution with cardiovascular sciences at its core, supported by multi-specialty medicine, advanced cardiac infrastructure, emergency and critical care, diagnostics, AI-enabled hospital systems, research, rehabilitation and international healthcare capabilities.

**Swasthya Saathi** is the digital healthcare and telemedicine platform designed to extend RashBhar HealthCare beyond the physical hospital through telemedicine, telecardiology, digital patient access, referral coordination, remote monitoring and connected care. The wider RashBhar HealthCare model explicitly positions digital health, telecardiology, home healthcare and research as interconnected parts of its healthcare ecosystem.

### Together:

**One project delivers advanced physical healthcare.**

**One project delivers connected digital healthcare.**

**Together, they form the foundation of RashBhar HealthCare's integrated cardiovascular healthcare ecosystem across Odisha and, ultimately, Eastern India.**
