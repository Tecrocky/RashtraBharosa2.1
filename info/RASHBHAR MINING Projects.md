RASHBHAR MINING CORPORATION
===========================

Projects & Development Portfolio
--------------------------------

RashBhar Mining Corporation is building a diversified mineral-resource portfolio across Odisha and Jharkhand, combining operating assets with new development opportunities in processing, strategic minerals and integrated logistics.

Our portfolio is designed to evolve progressively:

**Produce → Diversify → Add Value → Explore Strategic Resources → Integrate the Supply Chain**

This is reflected in five key projects and development initiatives.

01 — OPERATING
==============

Odisha Iron Ore Belt Project
============================

### Sundergarh Mining District, Odisha

**Project Status:** Operational**Operational Since:** Q4 2025**Mineral:** High-Grade Iron Ore**Target Capacity:** 2.5–3 MTPA**Primary Industry:** Steel**Technology:** AI-Guided Drilling | Geological Intelligence | Smart Conveyor Network

Building a More Efficient Iron Ore Operation
--------------------------------------------

The Odisha Iron Ore Belt Project is a flagship RashBhar Mining operation focused on the extraction and supply of high-grade iron ore from the Sundergarh mining district.

The project represents the company’s core operating philosophy: increase productive output, improve recovery, reduce avoidable operational delays and use technology where it can create measurable value.

With a target capacity of **2.5–3 million tonnes per annum**, the project is positioned to support the steel industry while establishing a modern operating model for RashBhar Mining’s iron ore business.

How the Project Operates
------------------------

The project combines several interconnected operating systems rather than treating extraction as a standalone activity.

### AI-Guided Drilling

AI-supported drilling and data-driven geological analysis are incorporated into the operating model to improve drilling decisions, targeting and extraction efficiency.

### Geological Intelligence

Geological information is integrated into mine planning and operational decision-making to support better resource utilization and recovery.

### Smart Conveyor Network

A smart conveyor-based material movement system is designed to streamline the movement of mined material and reduce unnecessary manual handling and delays.

### Integrated Logistics

Mining, material handling and dispatch are considered as part of one operating chain, helping improve movement from extraction toward the customer.

Production Focus
----------------

The project is designed around:

**Higher Recovery**Improved resource utilization and extraction efficiency.

**Higher Productivity**Better mine planning, equipment utilization and operational coordination.

**Reduced Delays**More efficient material handling and logistics.

**Reliable Supply**A dependable flow of iron ore for industrial customers, particularly the steel sector.

Environmental Approach
----------------------

Environmental responsibility is integrated into the project operating model.

The project incorporates a focus on:

**Low Environmental Footprint**

and

**Eco-Control Zone Compliance**

The objective is to improve productivity without treating environmental management as a separate activity from mine operations.

What Is Operational
-------------------

The project is currently identified as an operational project from **Q4 2025**, with its core operating direction centered on high-grade iron ore extraction, technology-enabled drilling, geological intelligence, smart material movement and streamlined logistics.

What Remains to Be Quantified or Expanded
-----------------------------------------

The project’s **2.5–3 MTPA figure is a target capacity, not a claim of current achieved production**.

Actual production, recovery percentage, cost per tonne, measurable AI impact, equipment utilization, environmental performance, employment data and safety statistics should be published separately once verified.

### Project Direction

**Operate → Optimize → Expand**

The project is not simply complete because mining has started. Continuous optimization, technology improvement and future production enhancement remain part of its operating journey.

Jharkhand Integrated Coal & Rare Minerals Project
=================================================

### Bokaro–Dhanbad Region, Jharkhand

**Project Status:** Phase 1 / Development**Phase 1 Target:** Q3 2026**Project Type:** Integrated Coal & Strategic Mineral Hub**Coal Target:** 1.8–2.2 MTPA**Strategic Minerals:** 15,000 Tonnes**Mining Model:** Opencast + Underground**Strategic Focus:** Lithium-Bearing Pegmatite Targeting

Combining Conventional Energy Resources with Strategic Minerals
---------------------------------------------------------------

The Jharkhand Integrated Coal & Rare Minerals Project represents the next stage of RashBhar Mining’s mineral portfolio.

The project combines coal production with strategic mineral development, creating a broader resource platform rather than a conventional single-commodity mining operation.

Its coal component is designed around a target production capacity of **1.8–2.2 MTPA**, while the strategic-mineral component targets **15,000 tonnes**.

A Hybrid Mining Model
---------------------

The project is designed to use both:

**Opencast Mining**

and

**Underground Mining**

This provides flexibility to match the mining method with geological and operational conditions across the resource.

Strategic Mineral Development
-----------------------------

A key feature of the project is the development of strategic mineral opportunities alongside coal.

The project includes:

### Lithium-Bearing Pegmatite Targeting

The project will evaluate geological zones with potential lithium-bearing pegmatite mineralization.

This is currently a **targeting and development opportunity**.

It should not be presented as an established commercial lithium mine or as confirmed lithium production.

That distinction matters, because accuracy is considerably cheaper than repairing a credibility problem later.

Water Management
----------------

The project incorporates an approximately:

**60% Water Recycling System**

as part of its sustainability approach.

The objective is to increase water reuse and improve resource efficiency across mining and associated operations.

Renewable Energy
----------------

Solar and other renewable-energy applications form part of the project’s operating direction.

The objective is to increase renewable participation within the project’s energy system while supporting more efficient resource use.

Dust and Noise Control
----------------------

The project incorporates dedicated dust and noise-control measures designed to manage operational impacts on workers and surrounding communities.

On-Site Ore Upgrading
---------------------

The project also includes **on-site ore upgrading**, enabling the operation to improve the quality and suitability of extracted material before further movement through the mineral supply chain.

Phase 1
-------

The current project roadmap identifies:

**Phase 1 — Q3 2026**

Phase 1 encompasses the principal components of the project’s coal, strategic-mineral, environmental and processing framework.

### Phase 1 Includes

**Coal Development****Opencast Mining****Underground Mining****Strategic Mineral Development****Lithium-Bearing Pegmatite Targeting****Water Recycling****Renewable Energy Integration****Dust & Noise Control****On-Site Ore Upgrading**

What Is Not Yet a Completed Operating Result
--------------------------------------------

The following should not be represented as achieved production or completed performance unless independently verified:

**1.8–2.2 MTPA actual coal production****15,000 tonnes of actual strategic-mineral production****Commercial lithium production****Achieved 60% water-recycling performance****Full renewable-energy deployment****Full ore-upgrading capacity utilization**

These remain part of the project’s development targets and operating framework.

### Project Direction

**Develop → Commission → Operate → Expand**

**Status:** Proposed / Development Pipeline**Project Type:** Iron Ore Processing & Value Addition**Focus:** Crushing | Screening | Sizing | Beneficiation | Ore Upgrading

From Extracting Ore to Creating Greater Mineral Value
-----------------------------------------------------

The Odisha Integrated Iron Ore Processing & Beneficiation Project is a proposed downstream development designed to extend RashBhar Mining’s role beyond extraction.

The fundamental idea is straightforward:

> **Mining does not end at extraction.**

A stronger mineral business also needs to control what happens after ore leaves the mine.

The proposed project would create a processing platform capable of improving mineral quality, recovery and product suitability while creating a more integrated connection between mining and industrial customers.

Proposed Processing Flow
------------------------

**ROM Ore**↓**Crushing**↓**Screening**↓**Sizing**↓**Beneficiation**↓**Ore Upgrading**↓**Customer-Ready Mineral**

Why This Project Matters
------------------------

### Better Recovery

Processing can improve the recovery of commercially useful mineral from mined material.

### Better Product Quality

Controlled sizing and beneficiation can produce material better aligned with customer specifications.

### Greater Value Realization

Additional processing can create greater value from the same mineral resource.

### Customer Integration

The ability to prepare mineral products according to customer requirements can strengthen long-term industrial relationships.

### Support for Future Production

The platform could support material from existing and future iron ore assets subject to logistics and project economics.

Development Status
------------------

**Proposed / Development Pipeline**

Building the Next Mineral Pipeline
----------------------------------

RashBhar Mining’s mineral strategy is not limited to established bulk commodities.

As industrial and technology supply chains evolve, strategic and critical minerals can become increasingly important to advanced manufacturing, electric mobility, energy technologies and high-technology industries.

The Eastern India Critical Minerals Exploration & Development Program is designed as a future-facing platform to identify, evaluate and potentially develop such opportunities.

Development Path
----------------

The program follows a disciplined resource-development sequence:

**Geological Targeting**↓**Exploration**↓**Resource Definition**↓**Technical Evaluation**↓**Economic Assessment**↓**Project Development**↓**Extraction & Processing**↓**Strategic Mineral Supply**

This progression is important.

Exploration is not automatically a mine.

A target is not automatically a resource.

A resource is not automatically a commercially viable project.

RashBhar Mining’s approach is to evaluate each stage before advancing to the next.

Potential Strategic Areas
-------------------------

The program may explore opportunities involving:

### Rare Earth Elements

Minerals with relevance to advanced manufacturing and technology supply chains.

### Lithium-Bearing Minerals

Resources with potential relevance to energy-storage and electric-mobility ecosystems, subject to geological and commercial validation.

### Other Strategic Minerals

Additional mineral opportunities with long-term industrial and strategic relevance.

Evaluation Framework
--------------------

Potential projects would be assessed through:

**Geological Potential****Resource Quality****Technical Feasibility****Infrastructure****Regulatory Position****Environmental Considerations****Commercial Economics****Strategic Importance**

Current Status
--------------

05 — PLANNED
============

Integrated Mine-to-Market Mineral Logistics Hub
===============================================

### Proposed Region: Eastern India

**Status:** Planned / Development Concept**Project Type:** Mineral Logistics & Material Handling Infrastructure**Primary Objective:** Mine-to-Market Integration

Connecting the Mine to the Customer
-----------------------------------

A mining operation does not create its full value when the mineral is extracted.

It creates value when the material can move efficiently from the mine to the customer.

The proposed Integrated Mine-to-Market Mineral Logistics Hub is designed around this principle.

It would provide a future platform for connecting mining operations with material handling, processing interfaces, stockyards, dispatch systems and transportation networks.

Proposed Supply Chain
---------------------

**Mine**↓**Material Handling**↓**Processing Interface**↓**Stockyard**↓**Dispatch**↓**Road / Rail Connectivity**↓**Industrial Customer**

Potential Infrastructure
------------------------

### Smart Material Handling

Improve the movement of bulk mineral material and reduce unnecessary handling.

### Conveyor Integration

Where economically practical, conveyor systems can provide more efficient movement between operating stages.

### Stockyard Management

Improve visibility, segregation and control of mineral inventory.

### Dispatch Management

Connect production planning with transportation capacity.

### Road & Rail Integration

Combine transportation modes according to geography, volume and customer requirements.

### Digital Monitoring

Future digital systems can provide greater visibility into inventory, material movement and dispatch.

Why This Project Matters
------------------------

The project supports several strategic objectives.

**Reduce Dispatch Delays**

Production should not be restricted simply because downstream movement is inefficient.

**Improve Inventory Control**

Better stock visibility can support planning and customer fulfillment.

**Increase Supply Reliability**

An integrated logistics system can improve consistency in customer deliveries.

**Improve Mine Utilization**

Better downstream movement can prevent operational bottlenecks after extraction.

**Strengthen Mine-to-Market Integration**

The project connects mining operations with the broader industrial supply chain.

Current Status
--------------

This is a:

**Planned / Development Concept**

There is currently no confirmed final location, logistics capacity, capex, construction start date, commissioning date or specific rail/road contract in the available project information.

### Project Direction

**Connect → Move → Dispatch → Deliver**
